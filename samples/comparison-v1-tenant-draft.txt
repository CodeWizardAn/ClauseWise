RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (DRAFT VERSION 1.0)

1. PARTIES & PREMISES:
This Lease Agreement is executed on 1st January 2025 between Shri Rajesh Sharma (Landlord) and Ms. Ananya Verma (Tenant) for Flat 402, Green Glen Layout, Bellandur, Bengaluru.

2. TENURE & LOCK-IN:
The lease tenure shall be for a fixed term of eleven (11) months. Neither party shall terminate this agreement during an initial lock-in period of 3 (three) months.

3. RENT & MAINTENANCE:
The monthly rent shall be Rs. 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand only), inclusive of monthly apartment society maintenance charges. The rent shall be paid on or before the 5th day of every English calendar month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Tenant has paid an interest-free refundable Security Deposit of Rs. 50,000/- (equivalent to 2 months' rent). The entire deposit shall be refunded within seven (7) days of the Tenant handing over vacant possession.

5. NOTICE PERIOD:
Either party may terminate this agreement after the lock-in period by serving thirty (30) days prior written notice.

6. REPAIRS & MAINTENANCE:
The Landlord shall be strictly responsible for all structural repairs, roof leakage, internal plumbing, and electrical wiring. The Tenant shall handle minor day-to-day maintenance up to Rs. 500/- per instance.

7. INSPECTION & ENTRY:
The Landlord shall provide at least twenty-four (24) hours prior written notice before entering the premises for routine inspections.
