EDUCATION LOAN SANCTION LETTER

Ref: MFPL/EDU/2025/00417                                    Date: 20 June 2025

1. SANCTION
1.1 We are pleased to inform you that your application for an education loan has
been sanctioned for an amount of Rs. 6,00,000/- (Rupees Six Lakh only).
1.2 This sanction is valid for a period of ninety (90) days from the date of this
letter and shall lapse thereafter without further notice.

2. RATE OF INTEREST
2.1 Interest shall be charged at 11.25% per annum on monthly reducing balance.
2.2 Simple interest shall accrue during the course period and the moratorium
period and shall be capitalised on commencement of repayment.

3. MORATORIUM AND REPAYMENT
3.1 A moratorium of course period plus six (6) months shall apply.
3.2 Repayment shall be in eighty four (84) equated monthly instalments of
Rs. 10,380/- commencing after the moratorium.

4. MARGIN AND SECURITY
4.1 The borrower shall bring in a margin of 10% of the total course cost.
4.2 A third party guarantee acceptable to the lender shall be furnished before
disbursement.

5. CONDITIONS
5.1 Disbursement shall be made directly to the institution against demand notes.
5.2 The borrower shall submit semester wise progress reports failing which further
disbursement may be withheld.
5.3 Any change in the course, institution or country of study shall be intimated
in writing within fifteen (15) days.
