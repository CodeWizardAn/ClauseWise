RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT (DRAFT VERSION 2.0 - REVISED)

1. PARTIES & PREMISES:
This Lease Agreement is executed on 1st January 2025 between Shri Rajesh Sharma (Landlord) and Ms. Ananya Verma (Tenant) for Flat 402, Green Glen Layout, Bellandur, Bengaluru.

2. TENURE & LOCK-IN:
The lease tenure shall be for a fixed term of eleven (11) months. Neither party shall terminate this agreement during an initial lock-in period of 6 (six) months. If the Tenant vacates early, the entire security deposit shall be forfeited.

3. RENT & MAINTENANCE:
The monthly rent shall be Rs. 29,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Nine Thousand only). Monthly apartment society maintenance of Rs. 3,500/- shall be paid separately by the Tenant. Any delayed rent shall attract a penal charge of 18% per annum compounded monthly.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT & PAINTING:
The Tenant has paid an interest-free Security Deposit of Rs. 1,00,000/-. At the time of move-out, a mandatory non-negotiable deduction of Rs. 29,000/- (equivalent to one month's rent) shall be deducted towards repainting and deep cleaning charges.

5. NOTICE PERIOD:
The Tenant must serve at least ninety (90) days prior written notice to terminate. The Landlord may terminate the lease at any time by giving fifteen (15) days notice.

6. REPAIRS & MAINTENANCE:
The Tenant shall be solely responsible for all maintenance, repairs, seepage fixes, plumbing issues, and electrical fixture replacements regardless of pre-existing condition.

7. INSPECTION & ENTRY:
The Landlord or their authorized representatives reserve the right to enter and inspect the premises at any time without prior notice.
